## Image, startup, and normal arming

The application built from wrapper commit
`37f2476c6984f15dfe2b9ecd2861321652497804` was read back byte-for-byte with
SHA-256 `98214708fcf511b3ccfb3a5511784791dda4166d87cd809102393e395d37f013`.
PR #51 merged the installed source lineage into `main` at
`dc3345dab633557de72e901627e31b7ffaea78b0`.

The policy change was one typed `FlightModeSettings.Arming=Yaw Right` update
followed by a single-object persistence request. It began and ended Disarmed
with every motor channel at zero. Persistence reported `Completed`; a deliberate
hardware reset then loaded `Yaw Right` again with `BootFault=OK`, no
Critical/Error alarm, and zero motor commands.

The subsequent arm proof used that normal policy—not `Always Armed`. It first
observed three connected low-throttle samples, held the configured yaw-right
arming gesture for 21 connected samples, reached `FlightStatus=Armed`, and then
applied the bounded 0.02 throttle fixture. Four nonzero `ActuatorCommand`
observations were generated by the flight stack, with a peak of
`[59,0,0,62]` on its `0..1000` scale and a hard evidence ceiling of 200. The
input returned low, 12 later motor samples were zero, and the transaction ended
Armed with zero commands and the receiver disconnected. It sent no settings or
persistence write.

The configured `ArmedTimeout` is 30 seconds. After that interval, a later
reset-neutral, request-only snapshot observed Disarmed, all motor commands at
zero, `Yaw Right` still loaded, uptime at 854,743 ms, `BootFault=OK`, and no
Critical/Error alarms. Unused optional sensor slots remained Uninitialised.
This is consistent with the configured automatic-disarm path, but the separate
capture is not represented as a continuous electrical trace between the two
observations.

## IMU acceptance and calibration boundary

The production transform remains `body=(sensor Y, sensor X, -sensor Z)`. The
closing stationary sample set contained 18 accelerometer and 44 gyro samples:

- accelerometer mean: `(0.0184, 0.6545, -9.4300) m/s²`;
- accelerometer population deviation: `(0.0218, 0.0152, 0.0272) m/s²`;
- acceleration norm: `9.4527 m/s²`, 3.61% below standard gravity;
- gyro mean: `(0.0152, -0.0103, 0.0314) deg/s`;
- gyro population deviation: `(0.0740, 0.0573, 0.0725) deg/s`.

The gravity vector implies roll/pitch `(-3.971°, 0.111°)`; live attitude was
`(-3.938°, 0.117°)`, agreeing within 0.033° and 0.006°. `BiasCorrectGyro` and
`ZeroDuringArming` are both enabled. These observations clear orientation,
sampling stability, stationary rate, and runtime gyro-calibration readiness for
an initial controlled attitude/rate flight.

No six-face accelerometer calibration is claimed. A single pose cannot separate
per-axis offset from scale, so the existing factory scale remains explicit. A
six-face procedure is a later precision-calibration improvement, not something
that can honestly be manufactured from this stationary capture.
